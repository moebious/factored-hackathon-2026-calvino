| 18 | 2026-10-02 | **Policy engine details (TSD-001).** The Route compares `needs_human` (not its complement) on two thresholds: at or above `escalate_at` goes to a person, strictly below `act_below` the agent path may act, between them clarify; ties go to the safer side.
